# About Me

I'm **Andrei-Cătălin Popa**, a Romanian astrophysicist-in-training with a passion for stellar physics, exoplanets, and reproducible data analysis.

I am currently pursuing an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's in **Astrophysics and Space Science (MASS)**, jointly enrolled at Université Côte d'Azur (Nice, France) and Università degli Studi di Roma "Tor Vergata" (Rome, Italy). The programme is delivered by a consortium of four universities across Europe, providing a truly international research and learning experience.

Before this, I completed an **MSci in Physics at University College London** with First Class Honours, where I developed a strong foundation in astronomical data analysis and computational methods.

## Beyond Research

I have a strong background in **science outreach and education**. I served as a demonstrator at the UCL Observatory in Mill Hill, where I led public observing sessions and school visits. I also co-founded an Astronomy & Astrophysics Club in my hometown of Constanța, Romania, where I prepared students for national olympiads and helped spark their interest in the cosmos. Additionally, I helped create TeachU.ro, the first free online tutoring platform in Romania.

I'm also an active **handball player and referee** — I officiate matches under the England Handball Association and previously served as president and captain of the UCL Handball Club, leading the team to a bronze medal in national university competitions.
